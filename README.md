# STARHUB 🌌 

## Overview 🚀

STARHUB is your cosmic companion, seamlessly integrating space-related updates into your Google Calendar experience while connecting you with a vibrant space community of like-minded individuals. 🌠

### Mission 🛰️ 

Our mission at STARHUB is to make the wonders of space accessible to everyone, providing a user-friendly platform that keeps you connected to the latest celestial events and launches. Join our global space community and embark on a cosmic journey together.

### Key Features ✨

- **Google Calendar Integration:** Effortlessly sync space-related events with your Google Calendar for seamless planning and access to exclusive celestial updates.
- **Community Interaction:** Engage with fellow space enthusiasts, share insights, and stay updated on the latest in space exploration through vibrant discussions and collaborative projects.
- **Real-time Updates:** Stay informed about upcoming celestial phenomena, launches, and space-related activities as they happen, ensuring you never miss a moment of cosmic excitement.
- **Customizable Alerts:** Personalize notifications to your preferences, ensuring you're always in the know about events that spark your interest and align with your cosmic curiosity.
- **User-friendly Design:** Navigate through space-related content and interactions with ease, thanks to our intuitive and enjoyable user interface designed for explorers of all levels.

## Getting Started 🚀

Follow these simple steps to begin your cosmic journey with STARHUB:

- **Clone the Repository:** Use `git clone https://github.com/STARHUB.git` to obtain the STARHUB repository on your local machine.
- **Install Dependencies:** Navigate to the project directory and run `npm install` to install the necessary packages.
- **Set Up Google Calendar API:** Obtain API credentials from the Google Developers Console and configure them following the provided instructions to enable seamless integration with your Google Calendar.
- **Start the Server:** Launch the STARHUB server with `npm start` to begin exploring the wonders of space.
- **Explore and Enjoy:** Open your preferred web browser and visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) to embark on your cosmic journey with STARHUB!
